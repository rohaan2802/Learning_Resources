Top Tech Careers (2024)

Software Engineering - designing, building and maintaining software systems to meet user needs efficiently.
Cybersecurity Engineering - strategies to protect systems, networks and data from unauthorized access, attacks and vulnerabilities.
DevOps Engineering - integrating development and IT operations, emphasizing automation and fast delivery of quality software.
Data Scientist - analyzes complex datasets with statistics, ML and programming to inform decisions.
Frontend Engineer - designs and builds user interfaces with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
Site Reliability Engineer - keeps systems reliable through automation, monitoring and rapid incident response.
Blockchain Engineer - builds secure decentralized applications using blockchain and cryptography.
Cloud Architect - designs scalable, secure, efficient cloud solutions.
