


                                                   ***********  3 Useful ShortCuts  *********

---> Windows + Ctrl + D    -----> Opens New DeskTop

---> Ctrl+Shift+T                ------> Open the last open tab in chrome

---> Windows+D                ------> Minimizes all the open tabs

---> Windows+Alt+R        -------> Screen Recording 

---> Windows+G               ------> You Can see Captures

---> Windows+X               ----> Press "U" 3 times to Shutdown the PC
